Women for Peace and
Gender Equality Initiative

We believe women and girls have the power to shape the future of their communities.

Women for Peace and Gender Equality Initiative (WOPEGEE) is a nonprofit, nongovernmental women's organisation based in Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria. We are nonpolitical, nonreligious and nontribal.

Our purpose is to equip women and girls with knowledge, skills and opportunities that advance gender equality, build peace, and protect and promote women's rights.

OUR VISION

A peaceful, equitable, inclusive and just society, where investment in women and girls accelerates social, economic and political development.
